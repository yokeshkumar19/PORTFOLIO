01 // The Problem

THE PROBLEM

Every day, students across multiple regional districts commute to the campus relying on college-operated buses. Without transparent digital tracking, their daily journey was plagued by uncertainty.

College students need a convenient way to access live bus locations, scheduled route breakdowns, and pickup details without depending entirely on manual updates or repeatedly contacting peers and drivers.

In practical everyday scenarios, buses face unexpected traffic delays, diversions, and variable departure windows. Commuters were forced to arrive at stops prematurely, resulting in prolonged waits in outdoor conditions, or risked missing their bus altogether due to lack of visibility.

Furthermore, drivers had to field distracting calls while operating vehicles on busy highways, creating both a logistics bottleneck and a safety hazard for college transit management.

"Students needed clear transit telemetry in the palm of their hands — transforming an anxious morning wait into a predictable, stress-free routine."

02 // The Solution

THE SOLUTION

RIT BusTrack brings bus tracking and route information into one application. Built with React, TypeScript, Firebase, and Capacitor, it connects students and drivers through real-time transit telemetry.

Rather than relying on disjointed messaging groups or physical paper schedules, RIT BusTrack establishes a direct, synchronized communication link between the driver's vehicle device and the student commuter's phone.

Live Bus Tracking & Location Display

Interactive map visualization tracing bus progress along regional highways, rendering real-time vehicle coordinates, active trip states, and dynamic ETA telemetry.

Bus Route & Stop Information

Comprehensive stop catalogs detailing sequential pickup points (e.g. Arcot Bus Stand, Walajapettai, Kaveripakkam, Ocheri, Perumpullipakkam, Dhamal) with live progression markers.

Bus Details & Operational Status

Clear transparency into assigned fleet vehicles (Bus R15 Kanchipuram, Bus R24 Arcot), operational status (Active/Offline), driver contact details, and remaining stop counts.

Student & Driver Interfaces

Role-tailored interfaces engineered for each stakeholder: an intuitive commuter feed for students, and a distraction-free trip management console for drivers.

Driver Location Sharing

One-tap trip initiation that allows verified drivers to broadcast device geolocation coordinates into Firebase Firestore throughout active route shifts.

Stop Requests & Notification Features

Temporary pickup reservation features enabling students to flag their presence at designated stops, alongside automated trip status notifications.

05 // Key Features

KEY FEATURES

Engineered around the authentic operational dynamics of college bus transit. Every feature directly addresses a concrete daily friction point.

Dual-Role Architecture

Seamlessly handles students and authorized drivers. Drivers trigger location transmission with one touch, while students receive immediate live updates.

Real-Time GPS Telemetry

Calculates dynamic distance and arrival estimates using live driver coordinates synchronized via low-latency cloud data listeners.

Sequential Stop Progression

Organizes multi-stop regional lines into clear, chronological stop orders so commuters always know how many stops remain before their stop.

Pickup Point Requests

Empowers commuters to reserve pickup points on scheduled runs, alerting bus operators to anticipated passenger loads.

Institutional Email Auth

Restricted to official college email credentials (@ritchennai.edu.in), safeguarding route data and ensuring only verified campus members can access the fleet.

Offline Route Fallbacks

Retains complete route lists, stop orders, and driver contacts even when vehicles are offline or in temporary network dead zones.

07 // Reflections & Impact

BUILT TO MAKE COLLEGE COMMUTES EASIER

RIT BusTrack was born out of observing everyday campus friction: students standing in uncertain weather conditions, constantly making phone calls to guess where their bus was, and drivers navigating heavy highway traffic while trying to communicate transit updates.

Building an Android application with real-time location telemetry and cloud synchronization offered valuable lessons in mobile engineering. It highlighted the importance of handling device geolocation lifecycle events, designing battery-conscious location streaming for drivers, handling offline edge cases gracefully, and translating complex spatial data into an approachable interface that any student can read in seconds.

Most importantly, the project reinforced my belief that the most impactful digital tools are those that solve concrete, unglamorous friction right in our own communities.
